## Voice (provisional)

- First person. "I" for opinion and experience, "we" for things the team built.
- Blunt first line that could start a conversation. A claim, never a question.
- Short sentences, light punctuation, plain words. British spelling.
- Energetic but not salesy: enthusiasm comes from specifics, not adjectives.
- Opinions are welcome; dunking on people isn't.
- One question to close, answerable from the reader's own experience.
- No hashtags, no emoji except `→` in lists, no "Follow for more".

## Caption pattern

1. Line 1: first-person claim or observation ("Every Sanity rebuild I've seen go wrong broke at the same moment.", only if he agrees it's true).
2. Line 2: the reframe.

4. Optional proof line, his own (the Sanity ambassador role is fair game).
