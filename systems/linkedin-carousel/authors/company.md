## Voice

- "We", plural and plain. Proof first: what we built, for whom, what changed.
- The writing rules in [copy.md](../copy.md) apply in full: British, dry, sentence case, no em dashes, no filler.
- No hot takes, no "I", no jokes at a client's expense.

## Caption

- Line 1 states the work or the result ("We moved Helix's studio to Sanity without a minute of downtime." only if confirmed).
- Close with a question or a pointer to the case study. No hashtags.
