## Caption

Most new sites break the first time marketing tries to publish something the designer didn't draw.

In my experience the cause is nearly always the same: a content model built around pages instead of the things a business publishes.

→ Fields named after layout (section3TextAlt) are the tell
→ Model content types before you design a single page
→ Name fields after what they mean
→ Reference content, never copy it
→ Put editors in the Studio weeks before launch
