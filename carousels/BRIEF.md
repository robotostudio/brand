# Carousel brief

Copy this to `carousels/briefs/<yyyy-mm-slug>.md`, or paste it into the request. Only the topic is required; the agent decides the rest within the system's rules.

- **Topic:** what the carousel is about, in one line
- **Account:** company | jono | sne (default: company)
- **Audience and their pain:** who it's for and what it costs them
- **Real material:** screenshots, numbers with sources, links, code, client names that are cleared to use
- **Must say / must avoid:** anything fixed or off-limits
- **Dropbox folder:** where to deliver (default: `/Roboto Studio/Brand output/linkedin-carousel/<yyyy-mm-slug>/`)
- **Deadline or posting date:** optional
